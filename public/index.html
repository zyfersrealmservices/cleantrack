Sort it before you throw it

Most overflowing bins start at home. Six habits that keep your street clean.

🟢

Green bin: wet waste

Vegetable peels, leftover food, tea leaves and flowers. These can become compost.

🔵

Blue bin: dry waste

Paper, cardboard, clean plastic, glass and metal cans. Rinse and dry them first.

🔴

Hazardous: keep apart

Batteries, medicines, sanitary waste and broken bulbs. Wrap them and keep them separate.

🔌

E-waste goes back

Old phones, chargers and wires go to an authorised e-waste point, never the regular bin.

🛍️

Carry a cloth bag

Less single-use plastic means fewer blocked drains after the monsoon.

🔥

Never burn garbage

Burning waste releases toxic smoke. Report open burning so the team can act.
